    What this is

    A read-only view of the organisation data behind NVA, read as RDF rather than as JSON. Search for an institution above, or paste an IRI such as https://api.nva.unit.no/cristin/organization/185.90.0.0.

    Every unit is shown where the data says it belongs. Where the data contradicts itself, the unit is marked and the contradiction is explained under Findings. Nothing is inferred from the shape of the identifiers.
